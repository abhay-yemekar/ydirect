# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

yDirect's public community should be constructive, curious, specific, and safe. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people;
- give actionable feedback with relevant context;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property;
- use inclusive language and assume good intent while addressing real impact;
- avoid spam, harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, or manipulation;
- follow responsible security-reporting practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private correspondence or credentials, deliberate disruption, repeated off-topic promotion, and attempts to pressure others into unsafe disclosure.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; close issues or pull requests; and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens safety, privacy, focus, or community quality.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@ydirect.tech](mailto:support@ydirect.tech?subject=yDirect%20Community%20Conduct). Include links and context, but do not forward unrelated sensitive information.

The owner will review reports in context and seek a proportionate response. Confidentiality will be respected to the extent reasonably possible, but cannot be guaranteed where investigation, safety, or law requires disclosure.
